A few helpful details

Good to know.

Is Pomo Day actually a native Mac app?

Yes. It’s built with SwiftUI for macOS, not packaged as a website. It uses native macOS notifications and keeps its preferences on your Mac.

Is the app free?

Yes. This version is free to use. There are no subscriptions, ads, or in-app purchases.

What does “ad-hoc signed” mean?

The downloadable build is signed for integrity but not notarized with an Apple Developer ID. macOS may show a warning on first launch. Control-click the app, choose Open, and confirm. For broad public distribution, a developer certificate and notarization are needed.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No account or network connection is used. Timer settings and the session count are stored locally using macOS preferences.
